Compose runs gunicorn with no whitenoise and no collectstatic, so nothing under a static directory is served in the real stack; a stylesheet link would be a 404 in production and a working page in development. Colour scales are Radix Colors 3.0.0 (sage, green, red), copied from the published package (@radix-ui/colors 3.0.0, src/light.ts and src/dark.ts). Steps carry their documented roles: 1 and 2 backgrounds, 3 to 5 component backgrounds by state, 6 to 8 borders by state, 9 and 10 solid fills, 11 low contrast text, 12 high contrast text. {% endcomment %}
