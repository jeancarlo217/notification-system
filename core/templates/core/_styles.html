{% comment %} The whole design system, inline. B14 inlined it because nothing under a static directory was served; B16 added the static pipeline (whitenoise) and kept the stylesheet inline anyway, because splitting it would turn a design change into a restructuring and costs nothing at this size. Static files carry the brand assets and the administration site's own bundle. Colour scales are Radix Colors 3.0.0 (sage, green, red), copied from the published package (@radix-ui/colors 3.0.0, src/light.ts and src/dark.ts). Steps carry their documented roles: 1 and 2 backgrounds, 3 to 5 component backgrounds by state, 6 to 8 borders by state, 9 and 10 solid fills, 11 low contrast text, 12 high contrast text. The brand hexes are anchored on the solid accent and its text, where they measure 6.21:1; the scale keeps the reading steps. Measured ratios: specs/dependencies.md, B16 section. {% endcomment %}
